## 1. Method

ESRS 2 requires two assessments, run together:

- **Impact materiality** (inside-out): which of our impacts on the planet and
  people are material? A topic is material if it is likely to be material for
  at least one person, not for the average investor.
- **Financial materiality** (outside-in): which sustainability topics are likely
  to affect our cash flows, access to finance or cost of capital?

Both feed one list of material topics. A topic can be material on either axis.

Thresholds used: material if **impact** exceeds our screening threshold, or if
**financial effect** is likely to exceed 2% of revenue or 5% of operating costs.
