## 1. Commitment and deadlines

We respond to a rights request within one month of receipt (Article 12(3)).
Where a request is complex we may extend by a further two months, having told
the requester within the first month.

**We do not charge for a request.** We do charge if a request is manifestly
unfounded or excessive, and only after explaining why.

## 3. Identity verification

Before disclosing personal data we verify the requester's identity. We ask for
something the requester would know and an attacker would not:

- the email address on the account, verified by a reply from it; **and**
- one of: the last four characters of the payment reference, the approximate
  sign-up date, or an account identifier.

Where the request relates to a child, we ask for confirmation from the parent or
guardian.

We never ask for a password or a full card number, and we never ask for
information by phone to an inbound caller.

### 4.2 Inferred data

Where the system derives a value about a person — a score, a classification, a
summary — that value is personal data and is covered by every right above.
Requests about inferred data are routed to the same owner.
